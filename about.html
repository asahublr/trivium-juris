About Us

Legal clarity when a dispute becomes business-critical.

Trivium Juris brings together complementary litigation experience, sector familiarity and a practical understanding of institutional risk.

The name points to the three foundations of the firm: three partners, three perspectives, one coordinated response. We believe a dispute is rarely just a case. For a bank, a company or a family it is an exposure, a process and often a question of reputation. Our job is to bring order to it: understand the facts, choose the right forum, and see the matter through to a clear outcome.

Courtroom depth

Experience across writ, service, commercial, recovery and appellate forums.

Institutional lens

Advice shaped around documentation, compliance, timelines and defensible decisions.

Local command

Familiarity with the courts, institutions and operating environment of Jharkhand.

About the Firm

Three partners. Complementary strengths. One coordinated response.

Origin
Trivium Juris was founded in 2026 by three National Law University batchmates who share a foundation in law, learning and long-term professional vision.
Structure
Each partner owns a distinct area of practice: public law and State litigation, service and institutional litigation, and banking, finance and recovery. Matters that cross these lines are handled together, not handed over.
Experience
The partners have appeared for the State of Jharkhand, public-sector bodies and universities, and for non-banking finance companies and insurers. Indivar Das Guru, an advocate and former Chief People Officer, joins as consultant with more than 33 years of corporate and legal experience.
Where we practise
We are Jharkhand-focused, appearing before the High Court at Ranchi, the sessions and civil courts, the Debt Recovery Tribunal and its appellate tribunal, the NCLT and other competent forums.

What We Do

Litigation, advice and dispute resolution under one roof.

We take on contested matters and the advisory work that helps avoid them. Public law, service law, commercial disputes and banking expertise sit within a single firm.

Litigation & appeals

Pleadings, evidence, hearings and execution before courts and tribunals, with appeal and supervisory remedies where an order needs to be challenged.

Advice & opinions

Pre-litigation assessment, documentation review, limitation checks and written risk notes that help a client decide before a dispute hardens.

Institutional representation

Panel and retainer work for banks, NBFCs, insurers and public-sector bodies, coordinated with in-house legal and recovery teams.

Settlement & ADR

Negotiation, mediation and arbitration where they serve the client better than a long trial, with enforceable settlement terms.

How We Work

A defined workflow, from first instruction to final outcome.

  1. Understand

    A preliminary brief to learn the dispute, the people involved, the documents and the result you want.

  2. Advise

    An issue blueprint and strategy note: the forum, limitation, evidence gaps, exposure, risks, timelines and decision points.

  3. Act

    We draft, file, appear, coordinate evidence and manage every procedural milestone.

  4. Report

    Timely updates on progress and outcomes, each with a recommended course of action.
