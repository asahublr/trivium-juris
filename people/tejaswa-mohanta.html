Profile

Tejaswa Mohanta contests matters on behalf of Central Coalfields Limited (CCL), the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) and universities, along with high-stake clients such as PayPal.

Tejaswa has particular expertise in high-profile service and employment matters.
